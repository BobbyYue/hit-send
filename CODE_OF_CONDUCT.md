# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than making personal judgments. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate it and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public report.
